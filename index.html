01

Research

My PhD asks whether cattle that live alongside people — especially free-roaming urban strays that feed on human food waste — are acquiring human-associated gut microbes and antimicrobial-resistance genes, a process I study as microbiome humanisation. Framed within One Health, the project compares wild, farmed and urban bovines using shotgun and amplicon metagenomics, with interpretable machine learning to separate the effects of diet, human contact and environment. Alongside it I am preparing HydroSink, a pre-registered meta-analysis of hydrogen-sink pathways across vertebrate gut metagenomes. I trained in zoology and applied bioinformatics, have built RNA-seq, eDNA metabarcoding and de novo transcriptome workflows for collaborators, and have taught undergraduate zoology since 2022. I am looking for a research internship during my PhD and, later, a postdoctoral position in a microbiome or computational-ecology group.
